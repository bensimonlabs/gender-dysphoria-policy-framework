## 📄 Description

*Modernizing the Diagnosis and Treatment of Gender Dysphoria: A Neurobiologically Grounded Policy Framework* is a white paper authored by **Joseph Ben-Simon (Bensimon Labs)**, offering a scientifically rigorous model for determining eligibility for irreversible gender-affirming medical interventions.

Grounded in research across **neuroimaging, developmental neuroscience, and medical ethics**, the framework proposes objective diagnostic criteria based on:

- Neurodevelopmental maturity  
- Longitudinal brain morphology  
- Multidisciplinary review structures

It clearly distinguishes between **supportive care** and **irreversible procedures**, with a strong emphasis on **clinical integrity** and **institutional safeguards**.

---

## 🧭 Purpose

This document provides policy leaders, insurance providers, and medical boards with **actionable recommendations** to align transgender health protocols with standards in:

- Public health  
- Bioethics  
- Pediatric medicine  

It is designed to inform **legislation**, **coverage policy**, and **clinical governance**.
